## How to interpret the synthesis

The professional and practitioner sources support the breadth of work a product might address. Vendor documentation establishes competitive overlap and some concrete integration questions. The priorities, proposed artifacts, pilot structure, and fictional example are our product synthesis.

No analyst interviews, customer calls, paid product trials, or live financial-model benchmarks were conducted. No market-size estimate, willingness-to-pay result, measured time saving, or investment-performance claim is established. Marketing promises were not treated as proof of reliable execution.

Some search results were not used as evidence: a FactSet research brochure returned 404; a GLG public-equity page timed out, so the accessible service overview was used; Bloomberg access was restricted. These access results do not imply that the products lack the relevant capabilities.
